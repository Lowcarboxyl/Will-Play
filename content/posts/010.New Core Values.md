As we formally enter the first quarter of our 30th year of operations, leadership has conducted a comprehensive internal audit of my life. Like in general. To ensure optimal performance, personal growth, and maximum shareholder value, we are sunsetting legacy protocols and introducing our core values developed over the past 30 years.

1. Take Due Care During Communications
External relations drive our long-term equity in the current landscape, no matter how much computers try to look like us. Every interaction with other entities should leave the environment better than we found it.

Policy Update: In order to achieve this major goal, we will no longer accept or initiate discourse regarding meh shonen anime. Any corrective action documents are to remain sealed until further notice. Opening them would only result in poor outcomes for both parties, trust me.

Official CEO Comment: 挨拶はきちんと。 (Give proper greetings.) When meeting people, it’s important to give a good impression! Even if, in your head, you just want to go home, leave that dour face behind, and enjoy talking to people. Give them something good to remember!

2. Adaptability
Failure is rarely a result of poor intent. As long-time FGC retirement home members are likely aware, the most important thing to surpass any challenge is the ability to adapt one’s skills to any situation. This core value, though it was adapted from the difficulties of fighting f-smashing Little Macs and dash-attacking Meta Knights, still stands true to this current era. As the owner of the Low Carb weekly franchise, champion of the Good at Games tournament series, and professional mystery tournament gamer, I believe that today, this remains our primary core competency.

Policy Update: For the continuing betterment of adaptability within our core structures, we have decided to permit "kinda vibing it out, you know" as an officially recognized baseline planning strategy.

Official CEO Comment: なるべく諦めない。 (Try not to give up!) Keep it light, and try something new if you can. No matter how much you try to maintain a lithe and flexible mind, the process of quickly recognizing, analyzing, and solving new problems is always going to be difficult. You just gotta try as well as you can!

3. Maintain Bodily Homeostasis
The most sophisticated transport crafts of today are completely useless if they aren't provided the appropriate fuel. Though eating food that feels healthy has been a long-time motto of our corporation, this new pillar also includes hydration, metabolic equilibrium, and mandatory sleep cycles for full operational ability. I am turning 30, after all; I can feel the joints cracking.

Policy Update: Though we will maintain strict adherence to all homeostasis protocols, our organization is now allowing the usage of "I'm getting old, man" or "I think I only got like, 6 hours of sleep" as perfectly reasonable justifications for lower motivation and energy.

Official CEO Comment: よく寝て、よく食べる。 (Sleep well, eat well.) Don’t underestimate the effect of being hungry and sleepy on your mood. And listen to your body! Proper vitamins and fiber are no joke.

4. Spiraling Out Mitigation
The modern environment is rife with excess information, recursive logic, and macro-philosophy. In order to prevent unexpected work stoppages from occurring, internal and external peer review will be conducted to prevent over-analysis.

Policy Update: In order to properly detect such recursive spiraling, any topic that starts to make me feel kinda bad and icky will immediately be escalated to a trusted board member for a sanity check.

Official CEO Comment: 悩んだら相談！ (If you are troubled, talk to someone!) Though it may feel like no one understands, that’s kinda the point. People have been trying to categorize the world for its entire history, trying to do it yourself is silly. It’s easy to miss something that seems obvious when you're stuck in your own head.

5. Maintain Whimsy
Though the usage of the term whimsy is new, the core operating principles of daily joy remain. A purely optimized system devoid of play is a system waiting to fatigue. Whimsy, fueled by unconstrained curiosity, remains our ultimate defense against operational burnout. As such, investments in expanding our knowledge base will continue unabated across all sectors.

Policy Update: However, to maintain high-value knowledge acquisition, short-form brain-rot media will remain strictly banned. Exceptions will be made only when provided primary context from a collaborator, or if the media features the rotating a e i o u cat. I love that guy.

Official CEO Comment: なせば大抵なんとかなる。 (You’re likely to succeed if you try!) This includes trying new things, attempting new fields of study, or even meeting new people. It won’t always work, but it’s always good to try, and an open mind is how to approach it! Even if you fail, failure doesn’t have to mean you gained nothing!

Standardizing these core values is not for the purpose of running a 100% accurate decision framework. Preparing a plan with no leeway is a plan that is destined to fail. While these rules may seem simple, they are the only reason our infrastructure can hold up year after year.

To be frank, our past 10 years did not exactly yield results in accordance with previous predictions. We set expectations too high, did not budget enough for daily operational costs, and failed to maintain strict compliance with work schedules. These failures, however, are excusable. It is the normal pattern of everyday operations as a human. Dwelling on the specifics of past failures will not yield improved results, and I can never be perfect, no matter how old I get.

On the contrary, as we look ahead to the coming fiscal year 30, leadership remains extremely bullish on our long-term trajectory. While global volatility and my silly brain will undoubtedly present challenges, I believe that our new systematic fundamentals have never been stronger.

To our esteemed board members: Thank you for your continued investment, memes, and patience during our innovative growth phases. I hope that our company can continue to provide adequate returns on your important investments. Like, tbh, I’m actually always worried about that, please let me know if I’m being helpful or annoying or too distant or something, I’ll try my best to listen.

In celebration, we will simply continue normal operations. Your wishes of well-being and support are more than enough.

Final CEO Comment: 無理せず自分も幸せであること。Don't push yourself too hard, and make sure you're happy too. A goal that I hope to make continuous improvement towards. I hope you will join me!
